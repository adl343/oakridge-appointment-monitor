Possible food patterns

Foods logged at least 3 times are shown, sorted by how often a symptom followed within the chosen window. These are rough patterns, not medical advice — discuss them with your GP or dietitian.

Backup & sharing

Everything is stored only in this browser. Clearing browser data deletes the diary, so download a JSON backup now and then. Importing merges entries and never overwrites existing ones.
